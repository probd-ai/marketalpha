# The living shape of `mean_long`

*Full-universe characterization: 1177 stocks with aligned truevx + prices, 2018-01 → 2026-07-03.
Generated by `meanlong_shape.py`; raw numbers in `meanlong_shape_results.json`.*

`mean_long` = SMA₂₂₂ of `truevx_score` (0–100 composite of structural votes + trend bias
on **relative** price vs Nifty 50). Everything below follows from that construction.

## 1. Physics — the finite boundaries

| Property | Value |

`mean_long` is a *slow pendulum*: it cannot spike, cannot gap. Any zone change is a
multi-month commitment by construction.

## 3. Movement grammar — phases

Direction segments from 21d slope (flickers <10d collapsed); ~10.2k phases each way:

| | Rising | Falling |
|---|---|---|
| Median duration | 60 d | 62 d |
| p90 duration | 235 d | 242 d |
| Median amplitude | +3.6 | −4.1 |
| p90 amplitude | +38 | −38 |

The shape moves in ~3-month directional pushes with occasional year-long marches.

**Critical inversion:** *during* a rising phase the median stock's concurrent relative
return is **−2.2%** (only 41.9% positive); during falling phases it is **+1.2%**.
This is the SMA lag made visible — `mean_long` keeps rising while the outperformance
that caused the rise is already fading. Concurrent indicator direction is a *rear-view
mirror*, not a coincident readout.

## 4. Lead/lag — how late is it?

Correlation of Δ₂₁ `mean_long` with 21d relative return at various lags:

| Lag of return vs indicator move | −126 | −63 | −21 | 0 | +21 | +63 | +126 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| corr | 0.08 | 0.22 | **0.36** | 0.20 | 0.03 | 0.03 | 0.04 |

Price leads `mean_long` by ~1 month at peak correlation; incremental forward correlation
collapses to ~0.03 after the move. The indicator is a **regime confirmer** — its forward
value lives in *state persistence*, not in fresh information.

## 6. The shape, in six rules

1. **Slow pendulum**: lives 30–80, speed-capped at 0.45/day, median drift 0.118/day; zone
   changes are multi-month commitments.
2. **Direction > level**: rising beats falling by ~2–3% rel/63d in *every* zone. The falling
   state is a persistent forward-negative signal (−4 to −7% rel/252d).
3. **The sweet spot is the middle of the ascent**: fresh 40↑/50↑ crosses carry the most
   remaining alpha (+6–8% rel/252d, ~72% abs hit). By 80↑ most of the move is spent.
4. **Bottom exits (20↑) are traps at short horizon**: −3.5% rel/63d; junk bounces don't
   persist relatively. Only by 252d do they break even.
5. **It is late by design**: price leads by ~21d+; entries on mean_long signals pay a lag
   tax — the compensation is state persistence (phases median 60d, p90 235d).
6. **Round trips are rare per stock** (74% never complete 20→80): wave strategies must
   harvest breadth across ~1200 names, not frequency per name.

## Caveats
- Universe = symbols with computed indicators (current N750-family membership) →
  survivorship tilt; absolute forward returns are inflated (bull sample: abs-252 baselines
  +4–8% even in bad states). Relative-vs-Nifty numbers are the robust column.
- Overlapping forward windows (subsampled every 5d) understate dispersion of medians.
- Single benchmark (Nifty 50); small caps' relative returns carry beta-to-breadth effects.
